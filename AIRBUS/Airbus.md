#airbus

Airbus SE (AIR FP) is one half of the <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">global large commercial aircraft duopoly</span> with Boeing, and today the clear leader in narrowbodies (~<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">55–57%</span> of NB deliveries). The group has three divisions:

| Division | Products | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Commercial Aircraft]] | A220, A320neo family, A330neo, A350 | ~3/4 of revenue and the bulk of EBIT |
| [[Defence and Space]] | Eurofighter, A400M, A330 MRTT, C295, space systems, MBDA (JV), cyber | Space restructuring → [[Bromo Space JV]] |
| [[Helicopters]] | H135, H145, H160, H175, NH90, Tiger | Steady cash generator |

**Equity story in one line:** <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">a multi-year volume ramp (870 → >1,200 deliveries) on a ~9k-aircraft backlog, constrained by the supply chain, with FX hedges and execution as the swing factors.</span>
