# Reading order

**Big picture first**
1. [[BNP Civil aerospace sector presentation Sep26]] — <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">the best single primer on the sector</span>: airframers vs engine OEMs, business models, 2029 forecasts. It is also the bear-ish Neutral voice on Airbus, worth reading against the bulls.
2. [[JPM European civil aerospace - Risks of an aftermarket downturn Apr26]] — the macro bear case at its peak (jet fuel +108%, Hormuz, possible shortages). Read it to understand what the market feared in Q2, and why JPM argued Airbus was a relative safe haven.

**The Airbus equity story, before and after the Business Update**
3. [[DB Airbus - Disappointing Q1, improved delivery outlook Apr26]] — the trough quarter explained (China admin, panel rework, WC build) and a clear 3-method valuation (SOTP / DCF / FCF yield).
4. [[BofA Airbus - Commercial set up for EUR10bn EBIT guide Jun26]] — <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">bottom-up Commercial earnings power</span>: A320 €12–13bn pre-R&D at rate 75, A350 ~€2.5bn, R&D as the flex item.
5. [[Barclays Airbus - The next chapter of the Airbus story Jul26]] — the cleanest read of the July Business Update: 2029 EBIT €12–13bn by division, €5bn buyback, 1c EUR/USD ≈ €250m.
6. [[BofA Airbus - EUR10bn is a milestone not the destination Jul26]] — <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">where the upside sits beyond 2029</span>: A350 at 16–18/month, A350-2000, NSA engine economics, FX assumption.
7. [[MS Airbus - Narrative shift yet to be priced Aug26]] — why the guide is a floor (1,066 vs ~1,200 deliveries, 1.22 FX, €250m buffer). Useful for building your own bull/base case.
8. [[Barclays Airbus - From climb to cruise PT raised to EUR260 Aug26]] — A320 profit per aircraft (~€14m, ~€16m incremental) and D&S turning from a risk into a contributor.

**Strategic debates for the long-term thesis**
9. [[UBS Airbus - Roadshow feedback Aug26]] — management on NSA timing, aftermarket ambitions (gear, brakes, nacelles), Pratt into 2027, and <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">the A220 possibly unprofitable without a stretch</span>.
10. [[JEF Engine aftermarket cash flows - Whose right is it Jul26]] — the engine economics behind the NSA debate (GTF loses ~$2m per engine) and Airbus' ~€22bn of extra capital-allocation capacity to 2030.
