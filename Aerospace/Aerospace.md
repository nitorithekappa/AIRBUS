# Structure
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Duopoly in large commercial aircraft</span> (Airbus / Boeing) with COMAC as a long-dated challenger. Demand is driven by traffic growth (~4–5% RPK long-term) and replacement; supply is constrained by engines and aerostructures — <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">backlogs of ~9k (Airbus) and ~6.8k (Boeing)</span> aircraft.

# Cycle view (Barclays, Sep-26)
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Next leg of the cycle is OE</span>: aftermarket growth moderates from exceptional levels while OE accelerates as Airbus/Boeing raise rates. Widebody is the clearest incremental upside.
