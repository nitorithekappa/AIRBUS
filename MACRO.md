# Airline macro backdrop (Sep/26)

<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">The key macro variable for Airbus today is jet fuel.</span> Oil has been at or above <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">$100/bbl</span> following the conflict in the Middle East (JPM tracks A&D performance "since the start of the Iran War"). Middle East flight hours fell <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">~48% y/y in March 2026</span> and only returned to growth in July/August ([[Barclays Tracking Engine Flight Hours Sep26]]).

**Airline balance sheets are starting to crack.** Deutsche Bank ([[DB Flight Deck - Fasten Your Balance Sheets]]) flags <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">airBaltic filing for Chapter 11</span> and another LCC seeking fresh capital. Three major US airlines are already trimming capacity. DB's analogue is <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">2008–09</span>: at the Q1-09 trough RPKs fell 9.1% y/y, FTKs -23%, and the quarterly retirement rate was ~0.7% (2x the recent average).

**But demand is still holding.** Global flight hours <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">+2.1% y/y in Aug-26</span>, 12.7% above Aug-19; average daily utilisation 9.1h. Barclays sees <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">no Airbus/Boeing deferrals in H1-26</span> and Q2 gross orders of <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">762 aircraft</span> (highest since Q4-23).

# Transmission to Airbus
| Channel | Direction | Comment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Higher fuel → demand for new-gen aircraft | + | Fuel burn advantage of neo/A350 vs older fleet |
| Higher fuel → airline losses | − | Risk of <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">deferrals / cancellations</span>, financing stress for weaker carriers |
| Higher rates | − | Aircraft financing cost; lessors (19% of Airbus YTD orders) |
| Earlier retirements | − for aftermarket | Mostly hits engine MRO names (Safran, MTU, RR), less Airbus |
| EUR/USD | − | See [[Hedges]] |
