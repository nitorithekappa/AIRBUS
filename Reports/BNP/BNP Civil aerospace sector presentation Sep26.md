**BNP Paribas — Civil Aerospace Sector Presentation, 18 Sep 2026 — Airbus NEUTRAL, TP €210** · Sebastian Growe, Stephan Klepp

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">"Clouded sky": BNP prefers engine OEMs over airframers.</span> The sector is ~flat YTD after the Middle East conflict, but structural drivers (traffic ~2x GDP, services growing faster) point to ~10% organic growth mid-term. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Neutral on Airbus: value crystallisation could take time, supply-chain risks could resurface, and the key catalysts played out at the July Business Update.</span> A 51-page primer, the best single document for the big picture.

# Key takeaways
- Ratings: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Rolls-Royce and MTU Outperform</span> (RR CMD in Nov as catalyst); Safran Neutral (limited upside after recovery); Airbus Neutral; Melrose Underperform
- Airbus leads in narrowbodies; Boeing leads in widebodies, but Airbus has narrowed the WB gap
- Airbus/Boeing 20-year forecasts imply 2–3% fleet CAGR, historically prudent
- CA segment: A320 family dominates; "steady push-out of targets, yet faring better than peers"
- Business models compared: Airbus OE/Services 82/18 vs MTU 16/84, RR 44/56, Safran 48/52 → <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus is the least aftermarket-exposed name</span>

# Relevant data
- BNP Airbus forecasts: sales <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€79.4bn 2026e → €110.7bn 2029e</span>; adj. EBITA €7.1bn 2026e → €11.9bn 2029e (8.9% → 10.7%)
- FCF: €4.0bn 2026e / €4.9bn 2027e / €7.1bn 2028e / €8.3bn 2029e
- BNP CA sales sit 1–4% below consensus each year 2026–29
- Airbus 2025 sales mix: civil/defence ~85–90/10–15; EMEA/Americas/APAC 50/20/30
- Upside to TP ~7%
