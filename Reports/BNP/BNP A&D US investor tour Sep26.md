**BNP Paribas — Sector report, 28 Sep 2026 — "Moin moin from the East and West Coast"** · Growe / Klepp

# Bottom line
After ~30 US investor meetings: civil names took ~50% of the time (vs ~20% in Europe). <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">BNP prefers aftermarket over airframers</span>; debate centres on oil ≥$100/bbl vs retirement risk and the next-gen single-aisle (ducted fan seen more likely than open fan).

# Airbus (=, PT €210)
- Investors asked about the <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">pullback since the July Business Update</span> (when the €12–13bn adj. EBIT 2029 target was introduced). BNP calls it a "travel and arrive" pattern: case has become <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">show-me</span>, as July/August deliveries failed to impress (idiosyncratic reasons)
- Focus almost exclusively on <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Commercial Aircraft</span>: supply-chain stability (especially <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">A350</span>), pricing/mix (incremental EBIT by platform), self-help from <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Spirit integration</span>
- Little interest in Bromo space JV, Defence momentum or Helicopters
