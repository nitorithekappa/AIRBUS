**Jefferies — A&D Weekly: Backlog Growth Led by Airbus, ~10 Years of Production, 24 Apr 2026 — Sector** · Sheila Kahyaoglu

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">OEM backlogs cover more than 10 years of production at current rates, and Airbus is driving the growth.</span> Caveat: in a downturn, a backlog without firm delivery timing matters less.

# Key takeaways
- 10-year backlog CAGR 2.2% to ~15,700 aircraft: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Airbus 9,037 (+2.6% CAGR)</span> vs Boeing 6,726 (+1.6%)
- Boeing's APAC share rose to 23% (from 15%); Airbus regional mix largely unchanged
- Backlogs beyond manufacturing lead times force airlines into longer-range fleet planning

# Relevant data
- Months of production: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">A320neo family 121 months at 61/month</span>; MAX 109 at ~44/month; 787 139; A350 134
