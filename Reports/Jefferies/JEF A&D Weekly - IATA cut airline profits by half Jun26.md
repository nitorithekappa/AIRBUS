**Jefferies — A&D Weekly: IATA Reduced Airline Profits by >50%, So Only the Strong Survive?, 26 Jun 2026 — Sector** · Sheila Kahyaoglu

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">IATA halved its 2026 airline profit forecast on the Iran war and fuel, but the industry stays profitable and demand holds.</span> Airbus angle: an A220-500 stretch is being studied.

# Key takeaways
- IATA 2026 net profit <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">$23bn (from $45bn)</span>, net margin 2.0% (from 4.2%)
- Revenue +9.5% to $1.17tn; traffic +2.1%; record 84% load factor; costs +13.1%
- Fuel at <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">31.4% of opex</span> (25.4% in 2025)
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus accelerating studies on a low-risk A220-500 stretch</span> (+5 rows / ~25 seats), pushed by Delta, AirAsia, Lufthansa, Air France; would keep the PW1500G
- A220: ramp to 13/month by 2028 remains the priority

# Relevant data
- A220 orders <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">1,109, 517 delivered</span>
