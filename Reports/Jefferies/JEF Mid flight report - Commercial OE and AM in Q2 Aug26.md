**Jefferies — Mid Flight Report: Comm'l AM & OE Fly High in Q2, 5 Aug 2026 — Sector (US)** · Sheila Kahyaoglu

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Q2 aftermarket and OE both well ahead of estimates, despite near-flat traffic.</span> This is the key evidence that the Middle East shock did not hit the industry P&L.

# Key takeaways
- Commercial AM <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">+15% y/y in Q2</span> (est +10%) after +17% in Q1, vs global RPK +0.6% YTD
- GE Services +26%; RTX P&W AM +25%, with <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">PW1100 MRO output +43%</span>; Safran Propulsion Services +28% organic in H1
- JEF 2026 commercial AM now +12% (from +11%)
- Commercial OE +14% in Q2: Boeing 171 deliveries (+14%), 737 moving to 47/month, 787 at 8/month
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">P&W large commercial OE -8% despite deliveries +15% (mix)</span>; GTF guided up M-HSD (~1,100–1,150 engines in 2026)
- GE engine units +26% to 659; 2026 commercial engines guide raised to ~+20%
- Defence primes +8% in Q2
