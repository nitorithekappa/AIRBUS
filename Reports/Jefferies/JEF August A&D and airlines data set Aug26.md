# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">A ~500-page reference chartbook rather than a note to read cover to cover.</span> Use it for historical series: deliveries, NB/WB market share since 1985, backlog by programme, production rates, traffic, airlines. US-centric coverage (Airbus not rated).

# Key takeaways
- Top picks: GD (primes), GE (diversified; "aftermarket fears overdone", Services 76% of CES), TDG (components), DAL (airlines)
- Boeing seen reaching +$1–3bn FCF in 2026 and $10bn+ over time
- Charts on Airbus vs Boeing NB share (A320neo gains accelerated by the MAX grounding and the pandemic) and WB share
