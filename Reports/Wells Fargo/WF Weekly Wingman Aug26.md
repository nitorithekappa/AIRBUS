- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">AM to decelerate into 2027 on high in-warranty flight hours</span> (more new deliveries, GTF aircraft returning to service)
- Defence: HSD+ growth vs MSD consensus; backlogs 2.5x sales; outlays gap 50% (highest since early 1980s)
- Top picks: ATI/BA/HXL (OE), RR/WWD (AM), GD/NOC (defence)
