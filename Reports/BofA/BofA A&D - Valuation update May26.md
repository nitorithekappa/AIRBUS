**BofA Global Research — Aerospace & Defence: Valuation update, Middle East-driven derating mostly overdone, 18 May 2026 — Sector** · Benjamin Heelan, Ronald Epstein, Lorenzo Di Patrizi

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Post-Q1 de-rating across European A&D looks overdone.</span> Civil aero held up despite the Middle East conflict, and the ~20% defence de-rating in a month is an entry point into a multi-year rearmament cycle.

# Key takeaways
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Civil aero 12m fwd P/E premium to SXXP fell from ~74% (start of 2026) to ~50%</span>; worries centred on aftermarket (jet fuel, airline capacity cuts), but Q1 aftermarket was resilient
- Risk is more to FY27 numbers than FY26; <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">widebody more resilient than narrowbody into H2</span> (flight-cycle data show narrowbody engines deteriorating more)
- Rolls-Royce: 115% of 2019 EFH in Q1-26 (BofA expected 110%), helped by the young XWB fleet
- MTU cheapest at ~8.9x EV/EBIT 2029; spare-engine ratio is the risk to watch
- Defence: ~20% de-rating in a month (Ukraine/ME ceasefire news, German contract award delays), yet demand lasts well beyond 2030
- Top picks: Indra and Leonardo (-18% / -25% de-rating)

# Relevant data
- EU defence: ~77% premium to SXXP (20y avg ~5%); <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">~11% organic growth 2026–30, +240bp EBIT margin</span>; ~9.3x EV/EBIT 2029
