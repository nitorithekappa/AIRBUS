**BofA Global Research — Airbus Commercial set up for €10bn EBIT guide, 5 Jun 2026 — BUY, PO €258 (from €255)** · Benjamin Heelan, David Holmes, Lorenzo Di Patrizi, Ronald Epstein

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">BofA expects the 21 July Business Update to introduce a mid-term Commercial EBIT framework of ~€10bn, tied to production rates (A320 rate 75, A350 rate 12) rather than to a year.</span> The value is in moving the market's focus from monthly delivery noise to earnings power.

# Key takeaways
- Commercial earnings power: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€14–16bn EBIT pre-R&D</span> at full rate
  - A320 at rate 75: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€12–13bn</span> pre-R&D (the core driver)
  - A350 at rate 12: ~€2.5bn pre-R&D (pricing and mix shift to -1000)
  - A220 breakeven; A330neo + Services ~€700m
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">R&D is the flex item</span>: ~€2.7bn today, rising to ~€3.3bn by end of decade; management may guide €3–4bn to keep flexibility. Major NSA spend more likely post-2030
- Market could anchor on <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">~€13bn group EBIT and ~€10bn FCF</span> by end of decade → ~8x mid-term EBIT, >8% FCF yield
- PO rolled to 2029 (stable production year); FCF ests updated for capex/tax
