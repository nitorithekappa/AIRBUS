#report #airbus

**BofA Global Research — Airbus: €10bn is a milestone, not the destination, 27 Jul 2026 — BUY, PO €258** · Benjamin Heelan, Ronald Epstein et al.

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">The €10bn Commercial EBIT target from the CMD was in line, but BofA sees it as conservative, with four sources of upside.</span> Model rolled to 2035 and now includes an A350-2000 EIS in 2032.

# Key takeaways
- Four upside areas:
  1. Widebody profit assumptions look cautious
  2. A320 outlook implies little profit-per-aircraft improvement despite volume leverage
  3. R&D guide sits at the top of the likely range
  4. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Target assumes EUR/USD 1.22 vs forward ~1.19</span>
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">A350 could go to 16–18/month</span>: widebody supply/demand exceptionally tight through 2035. At 17/month and low- to mid-teens margins pre-R&D → <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€3–5bn EBIT pre-R&D by 2032–34</span>
- A350-2000 assumed EIS 2032, ramping to 17/month by 2035
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">NSA: Airbus wants to rebalance OE vs aftermarket economics</span> → likely a stake in a future engine programme. RTX acknowledged preliminary talks
- Airbus and Rolls-Royce could align (RR wants back into narrowbody); an engine stake would add aftermarket exposure and could be a valuation catalyst
- BofA estimates sit ahead of the 2029 guide
