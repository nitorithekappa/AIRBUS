**BofA Global Research — Adding Airbus to 3Q26 best ideas, 1 Jul 2026 — BUY, PO €258** · Benjamin Heelan, Ronald Epstein et al.

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus added to BofA's 3Q Best Ideas, with the Business Update as the positive catalyst.</span> BofA expects a ~€10bn Commercial EBIT framework and a strong Q2.

# Key takeaways
- A €10bn guide may look light vs Visible Alpha consensus (>€10bn by FY29, >€11bn by 2030), but <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">the framework itself shifts the narrative away from first flights and deliveries</span>
- Q2 forecast: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">215–220 deliveries</span> (vs ~170 in Q2-25); <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Commercial EBIT margin 14% vs consensus 10.4%</span>
- A320/A321 mix broadly stable y/y → cleaner read on volume drop-through
- Airbus has guided ~€15m incremental EBIT per extra aircraft; BofA thinks A320/321 is higher
- Net cash ~<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€13.6bn in 2026, €17bn in 2027</span> without buybacks → buyback a likely BU topic

- EPS 2026E €6.91 / 2027E €7.89 / 2028E €11.3
