**J.P. Morgan — European Civil Aerospace: Risks of an aftermarket downturn are increasing, 21 Apr 2026 — Airbus OW** · David H Perry

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Downside-skewed view on European civil aero during the Iran war / Strait of Hormuz blockade.</span> The risk is highest for aftermarket-heavy engine names. Airbus should be relatively resilient on demand but is still fighting the ramp.

# Key takeaways
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">European jet fuel +108% YTD</span> even after a 20% fall since 7 Apr → airlines would need ~27% higher fares to offset
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Possible European jet fuel shortages by June</span>: net imports are 30%+ of demand, 70% sourced from the Middle East, inventory cover <40 days
- Airline profits cut: largest US carriers' 2026 EPS -42% to -80%; European FY1 EBIT -23%
- GE cut 2026 departures guide to "flat/+LSD" from "+MSD"
- Biggest financial risk: MTU and Melrose (GTF recall already weighs on FCF); biggest valuation risk: Rolls-Royce and Safran (high multiples)
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus as a "safe haven"? Perhaps</span>: high fuel favours fleet renewal, but (1) a severe downturn could trigger deferrals (not base case) and (2) YTD deliveries are weak
- JPM 2027–28E EPS sits 6%/12% below consensus; <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">a consensus cut would actually help, since Airbus has repeatedly missed aggressive consensus</span>
