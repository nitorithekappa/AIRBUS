# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Look through the weak Q1; earnings drivers are intact and adj. EBIT should rise >50% by 2028E.</span> A short, clean introduction to the long-term bull case.

# Key takeaways
- Q1-26 adj. EBIT fell to <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€300m</span>; FCF before customer financing <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€-2.5bn</span>
- Deliveries 114 in Q1-26 vs 136 in Q1-25, including a <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">25-unit A320 shortfall</span>; ~20 China deliveries slipped from Q1 to Q2 on admin issues
- Back-end loaded profiles and large quarterly FCF swings are normal for Airbus
- GTF shortage to persist all year; Pratt dispute unresolved
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Thesis: strong NB and WB demand, materially higher profitability and FCF by 2028E</span>
