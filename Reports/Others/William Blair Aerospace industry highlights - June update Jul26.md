**William Blair — Aerospace Industry Highlights: June Update, 15 Jul 2026 — Sector (US)** · Louie DiPalma, Bryce Sandberg

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Quarterly US view across aftermarket, production, orders and travel.</span> The aftermarket stays healthy as operators defer retirements, despite the US–Iran conflict whipsawing the stocks.

# Key takeaways
- Aerospace aftermarket index <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">+22% in Q2</span> vs S&P 500 +13%; the 17 June ceasefire lifted stocks, but its stalling weighed again
- Aftermarket managers saw little to no impact from the conflict; Q2 may be slightly softer than Q1's +17%, with a strong H2
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Boeing started a 4th 737 MAX line in Everett and is studying up to 70/month</span>, closing in on Airbus' ~75/month A320 target
- Strong Q2 orders on both sides, e.g. Lufthansa 10 787 + 10 A350
- Travel volumes -0.4% in Q2 (vs +1.5% Q1): higher fares and capacity cuts; business jet travel +4% April, -32% May
