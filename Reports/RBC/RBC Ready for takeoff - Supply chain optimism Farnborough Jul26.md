**RBC Capital Markets — Ready for takeoff! Supply chain optimism growing (2026 Farnborough review), 22 Jul 2026 — Sector (US)** · Ken Herbert

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Farnborough's real message was more confidence in the supply chain, not just demand.</span> RBC is "far from declaring victory" after 6 years of disappointments, but sees less downside-surprise risk. Airbus and Boeing are sold out into the 2030s, so it is all about execution.

# Key takeaways
- Orders: Boeing <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">180</span> commitments vs Airbus <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">119</span> orders/MoUs, ~300 total, well below the ~700 average since 2015 (backlogs already at record levels)
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus' 2029 outlook was "robust" and underpinned by increased supply-chain confidence</span>; well received by investors and industry
- RBC sees upside to 2026 delivery expectations for both Airbus and Boeing
- Boeing: Emirates won't take the first 10 777X due to extensive rework → potential pressure or charges
- Defence half of a record 1,600 exhibitors; shift from "exquisite" to affordable mass (sub-$2M interceptors)
- Aftermarket commentary very bullish; M&A pace accelerating
