**Morgan Stanley — Airbus SE: 1H26 Results, Strong Print Against a High Bar, 29 Jul 2026 — OVERWEIGHT, PT €227** · Ross Law, Marie-Ange Riggio

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">A 10% beat on H1 adj. EBIT that de-risks FY guidance and shows the group's earnings potential when it executes.</span> Defence & Space was the standout.

# Key takeaways
- H1 adj. EBIT <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€2.7bn (+24% y/y), 8.2% margin vs 7.6% consensus</span>; FCF €-1.2bn vs €-1.5bn consensus; revenue €33.2bn (+12%)
- Q2 adj. EBIT <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€2.4bn (+54% y/y)</span>, 11% beat; <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Commercial Q2 margin 12.3%</span>; Q2 FCF +€1.3bn vs +€985m consensus
- Commercial H1: sales €23.9bn (+15%), adj. EBIT €2.0bn (8.3%); volume, services and mix offset a less favourable hedge rate
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">D&S big beat</span>: adj. EBIT €487m, +84% y/y, 46% above consensus (7.7% margin vs 5.4%)
- Helicopters in line: revenue €3.7bn, adj. EBIT €240m (6.5%), higher R&D
- 2026 guidance unchanged
