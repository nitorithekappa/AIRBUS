**Morgan Stanley — Airbus SE: Narrative Shift Yet To Be Priced, 14 Aug 2026 — OVERWEIGHT, PT €255 (from €227)** · Ross Law, Marie-Ange Riggio

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Medium-term guidance and the buyback are a shift in the equity story not yet priced in; MS sees upside to guidance, consensus and the share price.</span> Shares are up ~35% from the March lows but still trade at ~11x EV/EBIT 2028. The catalyst path is lighter near term, so consistency of execution is key.

# Key takeaways
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Medium-term guidance seen as a floor</span>: MS's own Commercial forecast matches the ~€10bn 2029 target despite conservative assumptions:
  - <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">1,066 deliveries</span>, >10% below the ~1,200 implied by management's rate targets
  - flat list-price inflation, no change in discount rate, limited gross-margin leverage
  - R&D ~5% of sales, EUR/USD 1.22 (vs 1.15 spot), €250m annual negative "buffer"
- → Upside risk if execution tracks management's plan
- Buyback: signal matters more than size (~€5bn, small vs market cap)
