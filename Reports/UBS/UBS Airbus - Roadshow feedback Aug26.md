#report #airbus

**UBS — Airbus: Roadshow feedback (CEO and CFO in London), 3 Aug 2026 — BUY, PT €240** · Ian Douglas-Pennant

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Management is deliberately opening the next-generation aircraft discussion early, and wants a bigger share of life-cycle economics (aftermarket), well beyond engines.</span> Pratt relationship is described as constructive; supply chain reliability is improving.

# Key takeaways
- NSA launch towards end of decade; talking early to prepare suppliers (past programmes lacked foundational work)
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Life-cycle economics ambition extends to landing gear, brakes and nacelles, not just engines</span>
- Why move before Boeing: supplier readiness, plus emerging competition, especially from China
- Pratt: 2026 deliveries disappointing but in line with PW's late-2025 communication; <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">confident into 2027</span>; GTF AOG rates tracking at least in line; pleased with GTF Advantage
- A350 rate limited mainly by interiors; Melrose GKN Garden Grove issues not expected to affect rates; nickel alloy not a limiter
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">A350 rate increase decision tied to the A350-2000 launch decision</span>; costs could vary widely
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">A220 may not be profitable with the current two variants; a stretch may be needed</span>

# Relevant data
- Price €204.00 (30 Jul); market cap €160bn; shares 786m
- Model and PT unchanged
