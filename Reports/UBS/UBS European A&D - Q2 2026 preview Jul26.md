**UBS — European Aerospace and Defence: Q2 2026 A&D preview summary, 9 Jul 2026 — Sector** · Ian Douglas-Pennant

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">UBS expected volatility in civil aero into Q2, with risks skewed to the downside for Airbus and the engine names.</span> In hindsight it is a useful counterpoint: Airbus then beat on Q2 and delivered a 2029 target above UBS's expectation.

# Key takeaways
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus (−)</span>: UBS expected Commercial 2030 guidance of <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€10–11bn EBIT vs consensus €11.5bn / 13.4% margin</span> at the 21 July Business Update
- Melrose (−): 2026 guide OK, but 2029 FCF guide could be cut; MTU (−): risk to OEM sales consensus
- Rolls-Royce relatively preferred (sentiment already weak on EFH data); Safran balanced
- Defence: Saab (+) on the Polish A26 submarine order; Thales weaker after the F126 and SES cancellations
