**Barclays — Global Aerospace: Passenger traffic contracted 2.2% YoY in May, cargo remained resilient, 30 Jun 2026 — Sector NEUTRAL** · Milene Kerner et al.

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Traffic still below prior year, but improving as Middle East disruption eases.</span> Capacity was cut in line with traffic, so load factors held. This is the demand side of the cycle.

# Key takeaways
- Global RPK <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">-2.2% y/y in May</span> (vs -3.4% April); ASK -2.3% (vs -2.9%)
- International -1.6% (vs -5.3%); Middle East <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">-28.8%</span> (vs -48.1% April); Europe +3.8%, North America +1.0%
- Domestic -3.1%: China -6.2% (Dragon Boat Festival timing), US -1.9%
- Cargo resilient
