**Barclays — Global Aerospace: Tracking Engine Flight Hours, 14 Sep 2026**

# Bottom line
Global flight hours <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">+2.1% y/y in August</span> (+1.5% July); NB +2.7%, WB +0.5%. 12.7% above Aug-19. Average daily utilisation 9.1h (vs 9.2–9.3h peak summer historically).

# Key points
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Middle East recovery</span>: regional hours +1.9% y/y in Aug vs −48% in March, −32% April, −18% May, −6% June
- NB = 72% of hours: CFM56 46%, LEAP 29%, PW1000G 12%, V2500 11%
- WB = 28%: GE90 24%, GEnx 19%, Trent XWB 15%, Trent 700 10%
- By OEM: RR +3.1% (Trent XWB +9%), Safran +2.2% (LEAP +21%, CFM56 −7%), P&W +5.6% (GTF +27%, V2500 −12%), MTU +3.9%, Melrose +0.6%
- Relevance for Airbus: utilisation of A320neo/A350 fleets → aftermarket/services (FHS) and replacement demand
