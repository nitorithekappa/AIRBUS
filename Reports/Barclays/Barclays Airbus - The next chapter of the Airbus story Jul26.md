**Barclays — Airbus: The Next Chapter of the Airbus Story, 22 Jul 2026 — OVERWEIGHT, PT €220** · Milene Kerner, Afonso Osorio et al.

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Business Update marks a shift in the equity story from ramp-up execution to medium-term earnings power, cash and shareholder returns.</span> New 2029 EBIT target of €12–13bn plus an unexpected €5bn buyback. Given Airbus' record of conservative targets, Barclays treats the guide as a floor.

# Key takeaways
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">2029 group adj. EBIT €12–13bn</span> → ~15% EBIT CAGR
- By division (2025 → 2029): Commercial <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€5.5bn → ~€10bn</span>; D&S €0.8bn → ~€1.3bn; Helicopters €0.9bn → ~€1.2bn
- Mix differs from Barclays' expectations: <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">widebody contribution lower</span> (A350 launch-customer contracts, A350F ramp; management calls the A350 profit "sweet spot" a 2030s story), A320 stronger
- Industrial tone much more constructive than 6 months ago: <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">all 10 A320 FALs operational and A321-capable</span>, Spirit assets integrated, supply chain improving
- Industry may stay structurally short of widebody capacity even at planned rates → <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus evaluating A350 above rate 12, decision possibly before year-end</span>
