# Bottom line
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">'The next leg of the cycle is OE, not the end of aftermarket.'</span> Keep differentiated aftermarket exposure but add selective OE exposure, especially <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">widebody content</span>.

# Key data
- Civil aftermarket <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">+19% y/y in Q2-26</span>; engine aftermarket +24.1%
- Q2 gross orders <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">762</span> (Airbus 478 / Boeing 284), highest since Q4-23; YTD book-to-bill 2.0x; H1 combined 1,331
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">No Airbus/Boeing deferrals in H1</span>; WB backlog +5.6% YTD
- Airbus orders: Asia <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">51%</span> of YTD intake; lessors 19%; WB 13% of Airbus YTD orders (36% at Boeing). AirAsia 150 A220-300
- Deliveries: Airbus <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">237 in Q2-26</span> (record Q2; 114 in Q1) — pushed from Q1 by fuselage panel rework + China admin issues. 418 incl. July = 48% of ~870. Needs ~520 in H2
- Airbus historically delivers ~42% of annual volume in Q4
- Backlog end-Jul <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">9,358</span> (8,753 end-25); A220 backlog +22.9% YTD
- Cancellations YTD: Airbus 55, Boeing 25
- Parking 11% of fleet (vs ~9% pre-COVID); retirements well below historical average
