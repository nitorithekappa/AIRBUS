**Barclays — Airbus: From Climb to Cruise, PT raised to €260, 3 Aug 2026 — OVERWEIGHT, PT €260 (from €220)** · Milene Kerner, Afonso Osorio et al.

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Visible path to mid-teens EBIT CAGR and ~20%+ FCF CAGR through 2029, plus a €5bn buyback, should broaden the shareholder base.</span> Valuation rolled to 2029 with multiples unchanged; Airbus still trades at a discount to European aero and global OE peers.

# Key takeaways
- Original thesis (market focused on near-term ops, not long-term earnings power) intact, now with far more visibility
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Market underestimates A320 operating leverage</span>: reverse-engineering the 2029 framework gives <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">~€14m profit per A320 at rate 75, ~€16m incremental per extra delivery</span>
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Defence & Space is turning from a risk into a contributor</span>: end of A400M cash outflows, positive cash in 2025, low-teens EBIT CAGR to 2029
- €5bn buyback signals a change in capital allocation (cash no longer just reabsorbed by industrial problems)
- Execution remains the key risk, but the balance of risks is shifting

# Relevant data
- 2025–29: mid-teens EBIT CAGR, ~20%+ FCF CAGR
- Price €204.00 (30 Jul); PT €260 → +27.5%; 52w €157.42–221.30
