# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">For Airbus, Q1 was the trough, not the end of execution risk; Pratt still delays the FCF acceleration.</span> Aftermarket is running ahead of FY run-rates and airline behaviour is unchanged despite fuel and geopolitics.

# Key takeaways
1. Q1 aero-engine aftermarket growth ahead of FY guidance (MTU the exception); Q2 momentum strong
2. Middle East / jet fuel: no impact so far; no change in inductions or retirements; shop visits limited by MRO capacity, not demand. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Duration is the swing factor</span>
3. A320 production–delivery mismatch persists (panel rework, China admin). No deliveries in February; <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">China still ~20 aircraft below plan</span>; convergence expected from H2
4. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">LEAP de-risked, Pratt is the pacing item</span>: LEAP >500 units for 3 straight quarters (~60% of the A320 ramp); <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">PW large-engine deliveries -6% y/y in Q1 to 235</span>, needs +8–12% for rest of year
5. Defence divisions keep outperforming, providing group-level resilience
