**Deutsche Bank — A&D Flight Deck (weekly, last of July), 24 Jul 2026 — Airbus BUY, PT €232 (from €226)** · Christophe Menard

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Farnborough order intake was stronger than DB anticipated (>300 firm orders), extending backlog visibility despite geopolitics and supply-chain issues.</span> Engines and aftermarket were the strategic theme of the show.

# Key takeaways
- Anchor: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">SMBC Aviation Capital 200-aircraft split order</span> (737 MAX + A320neo family); Riyadh Air added 787s and A350s
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Biggest deals were engine deals</span>: IndiGo preliminary agreement for >1,000 LEAP engines; BOC Aviation up to 300 LEAP; major GTF selections
- Airbus D&S: record customer interest in the A400M — <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">178 orders, 139 delivered</span>
- Flynas firms up 25 more Airbus jets
- DB PT raised to €232 after the Business Update
