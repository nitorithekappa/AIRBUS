<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Lead topic: the $1.5tn FY27 US defence budget request, +44% vs FY26 — "a watershed moment".</span> Airbus news: China courting Airbus, and GTF Advantage deliveries from H2.

- Navy $150bn ("Golden Fleet"), Air Force $101bn (drones, AI), Army $60.5bn incl. Golden Dome $17.5bn; Space Force more than doubles to $75bn
- Beneficiaries flagged: BAE, Fincantieri, Leonardo, Rheinmetall, Safran
- Airbus: <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">China's NDRC chief met CEO Faury and urged more Airbus investment in China</span> → DB reads it as positive for future Chinese orders
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">A320neo deliveries with the PW GTF Advantage to start after June</span>
