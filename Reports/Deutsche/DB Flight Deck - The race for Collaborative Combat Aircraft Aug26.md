**Deutsche Bank — A&D Flight Deck (weekly, last of August), 17 Aug 2026 — Airbus BUY, PT €232** · Christophe Menard

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA) are moving from experimentation to procurement, and software may be the main differentiator.</span> Germany is set to be Europe's most contested CCA market, and Airbus is in it with Kratos.

# Key takeaways
- USAF awarded Increment 1 production to GA FQ-42 and Anduril FQ-44 (>150 aircraft by end-decade); mission-autonomy software procured separately from airframes
- Germany contenders: Boeing MQ-28 (with Rheinmetall), Helsing CA-1 Europa (with Hensoldt), <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus–Kratos Valkyrie (US airframe + Airbus MARS mission system)</span>, larger Airbus concepts, GA Gambit, Diehl FEANIX
- UK £300m StormFighter; BAE Brontanax first flight 2027; France via Dassault's UCAV for Rafale F5
- Airbus: technical specialists sent to the Air India A320neo mid-flight plunge probe (4 Aug); DB neutral for now, but notes a prior JetBlue A320 altitude-loss event
