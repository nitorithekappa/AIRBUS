**Deutsche Bank — A&D Flight Deck, 28 Sep 2026 — Airbus BUY, PT €232** · Christophe Menard — *tagged NEGATIVO*

# Bottom line
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Another manageable defect but less headroom for disruption.</span> Surface-protection deviation on lower-forward fuselage stringers of <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">~500 A321neo</span> (~250 in service / ~250 in production). Quality, not safety. Source: in-house aerostructures unit <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Stelia</span>. A321XLR unaffected.

# Why it matters
- Second fuselage issue on the A321 in nine months (Dec-25 panel issue took ~6 months to resolve, affected only ~20 aircraft)
- Adds to Spirit shortages on A350/A220 → <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">supplier industrialisation and QA not improving uniformly with the ramp</span> (60 → 75/month)
- Inspections catch defects earlier but do not remove rework, delivery phasing or working-capital effects
- 475 delivered through August → <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">395 still needed</span> to reach 870
- DB does not see FY26 target at risk from this issue alone; in-service fixes during heavy maintenance
