**Deutsche Bank — Airbus: A disappointing Q1, but an improved delivery outlook, 29 Apr 2026 — BUY, PT €226** · Christophe Menard, Sriram Krishnan

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Weak Q1 on EBIT and FCF, but the delivery shortfall is phasing, not demand.</span> FY26 guidance confirmed and the call context on China admin delays and the panel rework restored confidence in ~870 deliveries.

# Key takeaways
- Adj. EBIT <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€300m, 14% below consensus</span>; Commercial EBIT €81m (-49% y/y) on lower volume, mix, pricing, higher R&D and a 2c FX headwind
- Helicopters 28% below (mix); <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Defence & Space the only beat</span> (+17%, 2 A400M incl. Indonesia export)
- FCF before customer financing <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€-2,485m</span> (44% miss); working capital +€2.9bn, of which ~€500m from ~20 undelivered China aircraft
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">~20 China aircraft (admin) + ~20 panel-rework aircraft should catch up in Q2–Q3</span>; panel fix takes ~7 weeks per aircraft
- Pratt situation unchanged, compensation still under negotiation; current engine supply still compatible with guidance. No white tails in Q1
- WC build is a sign the ramp is real; FCF should turn positive in Q2

# Relevant data
- FY26 guidance: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">~870 deliveries, adj. EBIT ~€7.5bn, FCF ~€4.5bn</span>
- Q1 revenue €12,651m (+2% vs consensus)
- PT = midpoint of SOTP 2028E EV/EBIT €286 (Commercial at 20x vs Boeing >24x; D&S/Helicopters 15.3x), DCF €190, 4% FCF yield 2028E €202
- DB estimates: EPS 2026E €7.52 / 2027E €8.52 / 2028E €10.79; EBITA 2028E €11.2bn; revenue 2028E €101.4bn
- Price at report €166.10
