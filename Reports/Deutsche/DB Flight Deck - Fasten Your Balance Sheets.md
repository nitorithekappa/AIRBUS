**Deutsche Bank — A&D Flight Deck, 21 Sep 2026 — Airbus BUY, PT €232** · Christophe Menard

# Bottom line
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Echoes of 2008–09, but too early to call.</span> Jet fuel spike starting to test airline balance sheets (airBaltic Chapter 11; another LCC raising capital). Demand still strong vs 2008–09, but three US majors already moderating capacity. If pressure persists → <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">order, deferral and cancellation risk for OEMs</span>; earlier retirements → aftermarket pricing pressure.

# Airbus news & DB view
- **FAA waiver lets Boeing deliver 35 more 777F (2028–31)** → <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">modest negative</span>: extends 777F life, may weigh on A350F orders; capped
- **Ariane 6 technical checks** → 7–8 launches in 2026 harder
- **Boeing 737 MAX rate 47 taking "longer than expected"** (wing production); 737-10 cert "very soon"; 777X tests spill into 2027 → <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">competitively supportive</span> but Airbus has limited capacity to capture displaced demand
- **EVA Air** considering ~24 widebodies (777-300ER replacement) → opportunity, not favourite
- **New A321 delivery centre in Toulouse** (ex-A380 hall): delivery positions 8 → 16. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Positive for execution and cash conversion but engines remain the constraint</span>; supports 70–75/month by end-27
