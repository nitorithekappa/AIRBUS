**Deutsche Bank — A&D Flight Deck (weekly, last of May), 25 May 2026 — Airbus BUY, PT €226** · Christophe Menard

# Summary
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Airbus is cementing its grip on aerial refuelling: Italy picked the A330 MRTT over Boeing's KC-46.</span> MRTT is margin-accretive for Defence & Space, and capacity is being added.

# Key takeaways
- Italy: <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">€1.4bn for 6 A330 MRTT</span>, shelving the KC-46 despite KC-767 commonality (Boeing delays, cost, requirements)
- KC-46 increasingly isolated outside the US Air Force baseline programme
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Second MRTT conversion facility in Seville by late 2027 → throughput +40% (5 → 7 airframes/year)</span>
- MRTT seen as margin accretive for Airbus D&S
